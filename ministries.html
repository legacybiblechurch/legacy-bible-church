At Legacy Bible Church, we believe every person is called to grow and serve. Our ministries are designed to meet you where you are — whether you're just beginning your faith journey or looking for deeper community and purpose.

Our Ministries

⭐

Legacy Kids

Engaging, age-appropriate environments for kids birth through 5th grade. We partner with parents to raise the next generation in faith — through Bible stories, worship, and meaningful relationships.

Learn More
⚡

Legacy Youth

A place for 6th–12th graders to belong, grow, and encounter God. Weekly gatherings, summer camps, retreats, and discipleship groups that go deeper than Sunday morning.

Learn More
👥

Men's Ministry

Brotherhood built on God's Word. Monthly breakfasts, accountability groups, and service projects for men of all ages. Iron sharpens iron — grow alongside other men who are pursuing Christ.

Learn More
🌸

Women's Ministry

Community, growth, and encouragement for women. Weekly Bible studies, seasonal retreats, and connect events throughout the year — building lasting friendships rooted in faith.

Learn More
💎

Small Groups

Life is better together. Our small groups meet weekly across Redlands for fellowship, Bible study, and prayer. Whether you're new or longtime, there's a group for you.

Find a Group
🌎

Missions & Outreach

We exist to make disciples of all nations. Join us in local outreach — serving Redlands and the Inland Empire — and in global mission partnerships that plant the gospel around the world.

Use Your Gifts to Serve

Every member of the body has been given gifts to build up the church. There are countless ways to serve at Legacy Bible Church — from Sunday morning to weekday ministries. Whether you love music, hospitality, or technology, there's a place for you on our team.

🎵

Worship Team

Vocalists, instrumentalists, and sound techs who lead our congregation in praise every Sunday and Wednesday.

☕

Hospitality

Greeters, ushers, and host team members who make every guest feel seen and welcomed from the moment they arrive.

🎥

Tech & Media

Camera operators, livestream engineers, graphic designers, and social media volunteers who extend our reach beyond Sunday.
