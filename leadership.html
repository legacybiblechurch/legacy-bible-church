Leadership

Pastor Todd Barnett

Todd Barnett

Pastor

Todd Barnett planted Legacy Bible Church out of a love for God’s Word and a conviction that the local church is God’s primary means of making disciples. He preaches expositionally through books of the Bible, believes deeply in community, and will probably be the last one to leave lunch.

If you have questions about the church or want to connect before visiting, Todd would love to hear from you.

Rob Signs

Elder at Large

Rob grew up in a Christian home with a deep love for the church. His parents raised him in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

For 30 years, Rob has preached, taught, and shepherded men and women in the Body of Christ. He has a passion to see believers enjoy the experience of knowing, believing, living, and proclaiming the gospel of Jesus Christ. He continually strives to see those around him growing in their biblical knowledge, hunger for righteousness, and passion for proclaiming the gospel of God’s grace.

Rob is married to Amy, who has a genuine love for ministry and a devotion to the gospel. They have three children: Micah, Titus, and Zoe.
