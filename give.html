Common Questions

Are there any fees with Zelle?

No. Zelle transfers are free through your bank’s app. 100% of what you send goes directly to Legacy Bible Church with no processing fees.

How do I give via Zelle?

Open your bank’s mobile app, find the Zelle option, and send to giving@legacybiblechurch.com. Most major banks support Zelle natively — no separate app needed.

Can I give recurring?

Many banks allow you to schedule recurring Zelle payments. Check your bank’s app for a “schedule” or “repeat” option when sending.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Churches are recognized as tax-exempt under federal law and do not need to formally apply for 501(c)(3) status to qualify. Donations to Legacy Bible Church are tax-deductible as contributions to a religious organization. Consult your tax advisor for personal guidance.
