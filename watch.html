Common Questions

Why does the video say the stream is offline?

That’s normal outside of service times. We go live Sundays at 10:30 AM Pacific. Give it a few minutes and refresh if it’s Sunday morning and the stream is still offline.

Do I need a YouTube account to watch?

No. Anyone can watch without an account. You only need to be signed in to YouTube to type in the live chat.

Can I watch a service I missed?

Yes. Each livestream stays up afterward as a recording on YouTube.

What if the picture or sound is rough?

We’re a small church with a simple setup. Quality varies from week to week. Tap the gear icon on the video to lower the resolution if it keeps buffering. Please let us know if something is badly wrong. It helps us fix it.
