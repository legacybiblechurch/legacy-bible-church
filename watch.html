Common Questions

The video says the stream is offline.

That’s normal outside of service times. We go live Sundays at 10:00 AM Pacific. If it’s Sunday morning and still offline, give it a few minutes and refresh — we usually start the stream right at 10.

Do I need a YouTube account to watch?

No. Anyone with this link can watch, no account or sign-in needed. To type in the live chat you’d need to be signed in to YouTube, but watching is open to all.

Can I watch a service I missed?

Yes. Each livestream stays up afterward as a recording. The teaching is also posted to our Sermons page and podcast feed.

The picture or sound is rough.

We’re a small church running a simple setup, so quality varies week to week. Tap the gear icon on the video to lower the resolution if it keeps buffering. If something’s badly wrong, let us know — it helps us fix it.
