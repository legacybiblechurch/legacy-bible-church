About Legacy Bible Church

How We Got Started

Legacy Bible Church was planted in 2025 in Redlands, California. On Sundays we teach from the Bible, pray, take communion and share a meal.

We meet in a living room and there are about fifteen of us.

What We Believe

We hold to the historic Christian faith.

Scripture

The Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God. It’s our final authority in all matters of faith and life. We teach from the New American Standard Bible (NASB 1995).

God

There is one God in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. He exists eternally and is worthy of all worship.

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, died for sinners, was raised bodily and is coming again.

The Holy Spirit

The Spirit of God indwells every believer. He convicts, guides and empowers us to live for God.

Salvation

We are saved by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone. We are not saved by works or merit.

The Church

The church is the gathered people of God. We are called to worship, grow and make disciples together.
