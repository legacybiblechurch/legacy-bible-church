Come This Sunday Learn About Us

“make disciples of all the nations … teaching them to observe all that I commanded you … I am with you always” — Jesus

What to Expect

We meet Sundays at 10 AM. The service includes worship, Scripture reading, prayer, and teaching from the Word. Afterward, we share lunch and fellowship.

225 Fremont Street, Redlands. Come as you are.

Plan Your First Visit

Who We Are

We’re a small church that started a few months ago in Redlands. About fifteen of us get together every Sunday morning — we do a service, share communion, and then eat lunch together.

Pastor Todd Barnett teaches through books of the Bible each week. We use the NASB 1995. We take the Word seriously and try to apply it to real life.

If you’re looking for a place where people actually know each other, this might be it.

You’re Welcome to Come.

No need to reach out first if you don’t want to. Just show up. 225 Fremont Street, Sundays at 10 AM. We’d love to meet you.
