First Time Here?

Visiting for the First Time?

We are so glad you are considering joining us. Whether this is your first time in a church or you have been attending for decades, you are welcome here just as you are. Here is what to expect when you visit Legacy Bible Church.

Parking

Free parking is available in the lot adjacent to the church on Fremont Street. Look for the "Welcome" signs near the main entrance — our greeters will be happy to point you in the right direction.

What to Expect

Our services last approximately 75 minutes and include contemporary worship music and expository Bible teaching. Come as you are — there is no dress code, just come ready to encounter God's Word.

Kids Check-In

Our Kids Ministry (birth through 5th grade) meets during both Sunday services. Please visit the Kids Check-In desk in the lobby at least 15 minutes before the service to register your children for the first time.
